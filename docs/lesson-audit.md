# Lesson repetition audit — 24 October 2026

This is the historical 24 October trim, covering the 39 lessons present then. tr03 was added afterward. Use the [current inventory](content-inventory.md#lesson-sequence-and-companions) for today’s counts and the [curriculum audit](curriculum-audit.md) for broader findings and additions.

The owner asked for a **slight cutdown**, with lessons checking understanding and Practice housing repetition and drills. Reviewed all 39 lessons and removed 224 of 1898 counted exercises (11.8%). This pass removes only 5–7 items per lesson; it preserves the established lesson structure and most exercises.

All Points, Words rows, conversations, practice sections and kanji meaning/reading checks remain. Retained items keep their IDs, wording and answer keys. The 45 separate Practice sets remain unchanged and continue to supply repetition.

Removed the clearest duplicate noun/number substitutions, repeated sentence builds and finals that asked for a decision already checked. Kept useful recognition → building → application progression, meaningful form contrasts, conversation checks and mixed review. Bridge lessons keep distinct endings and verb-family work. Counts below include every matching pair, sorting card and cloze gap.

In Lesson 1, removed seven items: a second を gap with another drink; two extra request builds; two more availability builds; and two final questions duplicating the request/availability distinction. Kept both request endings, attention-getting, handover meanings, positive/negative replies, the transition from checking to ordering, both conversation exercises, kanji and mixed review.

Completion still requires all retained checks.
