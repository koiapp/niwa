# The learning loop

The owner set out the loop on 22 October 2026 and answered its first set of open questions the same day. This document is the reference for how lessons, practices, books and the garden's stones relate, how they are revealed and unlocked, and what finishing each one means.

The Learn portion is built: progressive stone reveal, lesson lists in teaching order, strict topic and lesson routes, reachable checkpoints and stone unlock notices. Practice is built on the loop: practice sets are data with their prerequisites and home lessons, grouped under those lessons, with first finishes saved and named. Since 2b the six optional lessons are optional practices, the Learn path has no electives, and a finished lesson part can name what it opened. Since Phase 3 (2026-10-23) Reading follows the loop too: every book opens on its own prerequisites and sits under its home lesson, a revealed book is a blank cover, and a book is completed by a clean attempt, with another try always open. Phase 4 (2026-10-23) wrote the content: every lesson is the home of a general practice and a general book, the earlier books are fitted to their chains, and the validator enforces both. The loop is built. [The app today](#the-app-today) records the state of the app, and [Changes made for the loop](#changes-made-for-the-loop) records the work. The sections keep these things apart:

- what the owner **decided**;
- the **rules** that make those decisions precise enough to check, each noting where it fills a gap the decisions leave;
- a summary of the **prerequisite map** of the shipped content;
- the few questions **still open**.

## The loop

Lessons are where the course starts and what it centres on. Each lesson explains the core and checks understanding with a few distinct exercises. Practice provides repeated retrieval, more vocabulary and variation; a lesson should not repeat the same request or availability question with several nouns just to fill a workbook. This pacing decision was added on 24 October 2026.

1. **Starting a lesson** reveals the practices and books that build on it. They appear locked.
2. **They open** as the learner works through the lesson or finishes it, and some open only after other practices or books are finished.
3. **Practices** layer more knowledge onto the subject. **Books** test it at points along the way.
4. **An end point** comes when going further needs a lesson the learner has not finished. Starting that lesson reveals the next set, and the loop repeats.

The garden follows the same pattern. Starting a lesson can reveal the next stone as an outline, and finishing the lesson opens that stone.

```
Lesson A ──┬──► Practice 1 ──► Book 2 ──► Practice 3 ──► end point: needs Lesson B
           └──► Book 1 ─────────┘
Lesson B ──► Practice 4, which needs Practice 3 and Lesson B ──► …
```

Book 2 needs both Practice 1 and Book 1. Practice 4 extends the branch that ended at Practice 3, once Lesson B is finished.

## Three kinds of content

| | Lesson | Practice | Book |
|---|---|---|---|
| Job | Teach a subject's core: its words, phrases and grammar | Widen how the lesson's material is used: changed wording, more natural forms, casual forms, variety by context, grammar differences, more vocabulary, and more niche situations of the topic | Help retention by practising a topic as a whole, in larger strokes: lessons and practices come together in a situation the learner works through from memory. A few new words can come in along the way |
| Lives in | Learn, on its stone's topic page | Practice, under its home lesson | Library → Reading, under its home lesson |
| Can require | Lessons only | Any lessons, lesson parts, practices and books | Any lessons, lesson parts, practices and books |
| Done when | Every item is right. Retries are allowed, and the first try is kept as the score | Every item is right, as in a lesson | One attempt has no mistakes, and the learner did not look at the text again during the questions |
| Form | Textbook unit ([content-standard.md](content-standard.md#a-complete-lesson)) | Question sets in the unit drill formats | Read, hide, answer from memory, reveal the evidence ([passage recall protocol](content-standard.md#passage-recall-protocol)) |

Every item of every kind is answered by tap or drag ([requirements.md](requirements.md#core-requirements-and-acceptance-criteria), INPUT-01). "Books" covers the topic passages and the unseen transfer texts in Library → Reading.

## Decided

### What each kind is for

1. **Lessons are the core and the start point.**
   - A lesson holds core material only, or expands on other lessons.
   - It requires only lessons, never a practice or a book.
   - If it needs something a practice has already shown, it teaches that again itself.
2. **Practices expand a lesson's subject.** They show how the lesson's material is used:
   - how wording changes;
   - more natural forms and casual forms;
   - variety by context;
   - grammar differences;
   - more vocabulary.

   All of it expands the topic and covers more niche situations. A practice can introduce these things.
   A practice may be written in parts, like a lesson, each part introducing its own words and their usage (29 October 2026, owner's request; [data-contract.md](data-contract.md#practice-parts)).
3. **Books help retention.**
   - A book brings lessons and practices into a situation the learner works through with the help of memory.
   - It practises the topic as a whole, in larger strokes.
   - A book can also teach new words, introduced along the way, in smaller numbers and at a lower difficulty than a practice. Practices are where new words usually come in bulk.
4. **Depth.** Every lesson has at least one practice and at least one book. In practice it should have many more. Each lesson is the start of a subject that can go very deep: practices build layers onto it, and books test it throughout.

### Revealing and unlocking

5. **Any prerequisites, all required.** A practice or book can require any lessons, practices or books its content needs, and it opens only once all of them are met.
6. **Starting reveals.** Starting to work through a lesson reveals the next practices and books, still locked. Practices and books reveal the items that build on them in the same way.
7. **Progress unlocks.** Items open as the learner works through a lesson or finishes it, or as they finish other practices and books.
8. **Locked means closed, strictly.** Anything locked cannot be opened, even when it is visible. This covers lessons, practices, books and stones alike, by any route. A locked practice or book also shows no list of requirements.
   - A revealed practice or book shows its shape only, with no name: a koi silhouette in Practice, a blank book cover in Reading.
   - Its name appears once it opens, just as an outline stone gets its kanji.
9. **The learner goes to it.** Once open, a practice is reached in Practice and a book in Library → Reading. Nothing opens by itself and nothing redirects.
10. **Achievable.** Every prerequisite is mapped, and nothing can stay locked for good.

### Finishing and replaying

11. **Completion.**
    - A lesson is done when every item is right, as now.
    - A practice is done the same way, like any exercise set.
    - A book is completed only when an attempt has no mistakes and the text was not looked at again during the questions.
12. **Replays.**
    - A practice or book can be done again.
    - A replay keeps its own results and never relocks, reopens or resets anything.
    - Only the first finish opens the items that build on it.

    A stone can open as a follow-up to another stone, so one lesson can open more than one stone.

### Optional content

14. **Optional content is practices and books, never lessons.** *(Owner, 22 October 2026. This replaces the earlier elective stones.)*
    - Some activities are meant to be more optional than others, and creating optional content is welcome.
    - Optional content is for niche, less general situations. Dating and relationships is nicher than going to a restaurant.
    - Every lesson is part of the core flow. Anything optional is an **optional practice**, which can unlock **optional books**. There are no optional lessons and no elective stones.
    - This is a light change, not an overhaul. Optional practices and books follow the same loop as every other practice and book ([rule 12](#rules)).
    - What became optional (built in 2b, 2026-10-22):
      - *Without onions, please* (once r05, Restaurants) is an optional practice under *Build an order* (r03).
      - *Sizes and colors* (once s03, Shopping) is an optional practice under *This one or that one?* (s02).
      - Dating and relationships' four lessons (once d01–d04) are optional practices under *Invite someone* (pl02), and its two books (pd01, pd02) are optional books.

### Kanji cards

It is not a loop item: it has no home lesson, no `requires` and no practice file, it is never a prerequisite, and Practice has no group for it. The learner reads a set's kanji one at a time on its board, then practises the set (five questions a kanji), and the first finished practice opens the next set, whatever the score. Mastery is undefined for now, and a cumulative test mode is left for later ([data-contract.md](data-contract.md#kanji-sets)). The personal profile keeps the round above.

## Rules

These rules make the decisions checkable. A rule marked *(fills a gap)* settles something the decisions leave unsaid; the owner can overturn any of them.

1. **States.** Every practice, book and stone is in exactly one state:
   - *hidden*: not shown;
   - *revealed*: shown as a shape only, with no name and no requirements text, locked and not clickable;
   - *open*;
   - *started*;
   - *finished* (for a book, *completed*).

   Lessons keep the states they have on topic pages: done (a check), in progress (a ring showing the whole-number percent of items right, `rowProgress` in `learn.mjs`), open (an arrow; also a started lesson at 0%), or locked (the greyed title only, no reason line; 2026-10-29).
2. **What a prerequisite can name.** *(fills a gap: decision 7, "as you progress through" a lesson)* A prerequisite is one of:
   - a lesson, met when it is finished;
   - a lesson part (the words, one point, the conversation or the まとめ; see [data-contract.md](data-contract.md#references)), met when every item in it is right;
   - a practice, met when it is finished;
   - a book, met when it is completed.

   Checkpoints and a book's delayed recall cannot be prerequisites.
3. **Revealed and open.** *(fills a gap: reveal on every prerequisite, not just one)*
   - An item is revealed once **every** direct prerequisite has been started. A lesson part counts as started when its lesson is.
   - It is open once every direct prerequisite is met.
   - Requiring all of them, not just one, keeps "revealed" meaning "next", so a book that needs two topics does not sit locked from the first lesson onward.
   - **One exception (owner, 2026-10-22):** the first koi, *Restaurant review: a full visit*, shows as a silhouette from the very start, before any of its lessons is started. No other item does. *Everyday dishes* stays hidden until the restaurant review is started. The data flag is `revealed: 'start'`. It only makes the item visible early and never opens it.
   - Started and finished items stay open whatever happens later, to the content or to saved progress.
4. **Stones follow their lessons.** *(fills a gap)*
   - A stone has no prerequisites of its own. It is open when any of its lessons is open.
   - It is in outline when any of its lessons has every prerequisite started but none of its lessons is open. Otherwise it is hidden.
   - A topic with no playable lessons stays hidden, so no stone is ever shown that can never open.
   - Every stone holds lessons only, and every lesson is core (decision 14).
   - A topic whose content is all optional has no stone of its own. Its node stays in the catalogue, hidden, and its practices and books live in Practice and Reading under their home lessons.
5. **Home lesson.** *(fills a gap: one per item)*
   - Every practice and book has exactly one home lesson. It is the lesson the item expands or tests, and it must be in the item's chain.
   - Practice and Reading list items under their home lesson, ordered by the home lesson's stone.
6. **Prerequisites follow content.**
   - A practice or book uses only the words and patterns its chain teaches, plus whatever it introduces itself.
   - A practice introduces new words in bulk, along with new forms, wordings and grammar differences (decision 2).
   - A book introduces only a few new words, each explained where it first appears (decision 3).
   - *(fills a gap)* "Fewer and easier" for a book means:
     - words and set phrases only, never a new grammar pattern;
     - at least 95% of its running words taught by its chain. That is the known-word coverage in [curriculum.md](curriculum.md#passage-recall-ladder).
   - A word that a practice or book introduces counts as taught for everything that requires that item.
   - A lesson never relies on material from a practice or book; it teaches the material again instead (decision 1).
   - *(fills a gap: what "relies on" means for words)* A lesson's drills must not depend on a word that only practices or books teach.
     - Drills are the items the learner answers: in every set, including the まとめ and its returned items, and the lesson's transfer question.
     - A word a drill uses that a practice or book teaches must also be taught by the lesson's chain: its own Words table, or that of a lesson in its `path.requires`.
     - The rest of the lesson is display: the conversation, the points and their examples, Focus, each set's solved example (例), kanji intro lines and feedback. Display may show such a word with its gloss, as it shows the course's other context words.
     - The validator fails on a drill that breaks this ([data-contract.md](data-contract.md#validator)). It has held for the shipped lessons since 23 October 2026.
       - *(refined 29 October 2026)* It fails only when a practice or book that teaches the word is homed at the drilling lesson itself or at a lesson at or after it in the path's teaching order (a home not on the path counts as after): the lesson would then rely on its own or a later expansion. When every teacher is homed at an earlier lesson, the validator warns instead (`lesson l03p2: its drills use なんばん (nanban), taught earlier by practice phone-and-months but not by the lesson's chain: l03p2-C2`), so a person can decide whether the lesson should teach the word. The reason: practices now teach vocabulary in bulk, a word an earlier practice teaches is available before the lesson, and the lesson already used it untaught as a context word before the practice taught it.
7. **Completion can always be reached.**
   - A wrong item in a lesson or practice can be retried until it is right.
   - A book can be attempted again at any time, with no limit and no wait. Each attempt starts with a fresh read, and the questions come in a new order.
   - For an unseen text, only the first attempt counts as transfer evidence, but any clean attempt completes it.
8. **Delayed recall never gates.** Due passage recall is available in Reading. Nothing waits on those results. Unseen texts open on their own requires; the former delayed-recall gate remains removed.
9. **No route into a locked item** (decision 8).
   - A locked lesson, practice, book or stone is never a link or a button anywhere: not on topic pages, in Practice, in Reading or the garden, on finish screens, on word cards or flashcard source lines, or in checkpoint results.
   - Wherever a line would otherwise point to a locked item, such as a word's source lesson on a flashcard, it shows as plain text.
   - *(fills a gap)* A direct URL, bookmark or saved link to a locked item opens nothing. The address replaces itself with the place where the item lives (its stone's topic page, Practice, or Library → Reading), leaving no history entry, as old `#today` links do. No locked page exists, for lessons or anything else.
   - Started and finished items are open (rule 3), so this never shuts a learner out of work they have begun.
10. **Every unlock is named where it happens.**
   - The finish screen of a lesson, practice or book names each practice, book, lesson and stone it opened, and where each lives.
   - An item opened by a lesson part is named in one line at the end of that section.
   - These are text only, with no links.
   - *(since 2026-10-28)* Each unlock, and each kanji word met for the first time, is also announced in a small notice popup (`notices.mjs`; [data-contract.md](data-contract.md#notices)) saying what opened and where it lives. A notice is text only too: tapping it only dismisses it.
11. **The validator proves it.** `tools/validate.mjs` fails on any of these (since Phase 1, 2026-10-22):
    - a cycle;
    - a prerequisite that is missing or names an outline-only lesson;
    - a practice or book whose chain has no lesson;
    - a lesson that requires a practice or book;
    - a general practice or book whose chain holds an optional one (rule 12);
    - a home lesson outside its item's chain;
    - any item still locked after a simulated learner, starting from no progress, has repeatedly finished everything open;
    - a lesson whose drill items use a word that practices or books teach but the lesson's own chain does not (rule 6, since 23 October 2026), when a teaching practice or book is homed at that lesson, a later one or a lesson off the path; since 29 October 2026, when every teacher is homed at an earlier lesson, it is a warning instead (`Loop: lesson …: its drills use … taught earlier by practice … but not by the lesson's chain`). Display text (conversation, examples, solved 例, feedback) may still show such a word with its gloss.

    It also fails on a lesson that is not the home of at least one general practice and one general book, on a book word that its chain does not teach and the book does not introduce, and on a book that introduces more than 5% of its word occurrences (rule 6). These three were warnings until the content was written; they fail the validator since Phase 4 (2026-10-23).
12. **Optional practices and books.**
    - A practice or book is optional when it is marked optional.
    - Optional content only leads to optional content. An item whose chain holds an optional practice or book must be optional itself. Skipping every optional item therefore never blocks anything general.
    - *(fills a gap)* Optional items sit in their home lesson's group after the general ones, with a small *Optional* label.
    - Only general practices and books count toward each lesson's minimum of one practice and one book (decision 4). Optional ones come on top.
    - Apart from that, optional items follow every other rule: prerequisites, reveal, strict locks, completion and replays.
