## Suggested content additions

The two High additions are implemented. Remaining rows are proposed authoring batches. New lessons must include general practice, an accessible book, checkpoint coverage and prerequisite chains that teach every assessed word and form.

| 1 | High | A price card and a shared-interest exchange | Implemented as ps05 under s04 and pi07 under i05; each requires only its home lesson | Closes the delayed-reading gaps while retaining cumulative texts | Five immediate and five different delayed questions per book |

| 5 | Medium | Make or change an appointment | First Appointments lesson, adapting its existing catalogue titles after the needed time/planning language | Opens a new practical venue using established dates, durations, clarification and confirmation | Retain date, start time, place and changed detail in a booking confirmation |
| 6 | Medium | Household task or building notice | Use the existing Home life titles after bc01–bc03 and the required schedule language | Adds everyday home content beyond locating objects/people and describing rooms | Identify who does what, when, and which exception applies |
| 7 | Low | Wrong item or missing belonging | Start Fixing problems with its existing catalogue titles after the necessary requests and past-description language | Transfers restaurant/shop repair into a new problem-solving situation | State the mismatch, ask for a feasible next step and confirm the outcome |

Task-based additions fit the existing can-do approach: the [JF Standard](https://www.jfstandard.jpf.go.jp/summaryen/ja/render.do) distinguishes accomplishing communicative tasks from merely knowing vocabulary or grammar. [Irodori](https://www.irodori.jpf.go.jp/en/about.html) provides a relevant everyday-life reference. The proposed order above is an editorial choice for Niwa, not an official sequence or a CEFR/JLPT equivalence.

## Structure worth preserving

Keep the early restaurant/people/shopping alternation, explicit grammar bridges, contextual kanji words, first-response evidence, support-aware reading, changed-context checkpoints and optional Dating branch. Every current lesson has a general practice and a general book; the dependency graph is reachable and no general item is gated by optional content. The app's tap/drag results measure written recognition and controlled building. Listening, speaking and independent conversational assessment remain outside the implemented scope.

After a content change, rebuild the relevant JSON from its authoring source, run `node tools/content-inventory.mjs --write`, review this audit's affected rows and the explanatory docs, then run `node tools/validate.mjs`. The validator compares the generated inventory with current data and fails on drift. It cannot decide whether a prose recommendation is resolved: update its status only after the corresponding content or behavior actually exists. Checkpoint the change with the workspace's `checkpoint.mjs`.

## High implementation details

The teaching baseline retains its original pinned source commit and now has zero entries; validation never creates exemptions. The two exceptions are an entirely English no-reply decision and copying a displayed number. Coverage checks do not replace Japanese educator review.

The batch adds tr00 “Read a station sign”, first-order and station-exits practice, and four books: p11 “A small order for two”, ps05 “One apple or a set?”, pi07 “Same drink, different days”, and pt00 “Two exits on the board”.
